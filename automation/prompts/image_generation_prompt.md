# AI Image Generation Prompt Guide: Califnco Marketing Imagery

## Objective
Generate clean, photorealistic, premium commercial product photography for water purifiers, filtration components, and commercial RO plants without text artifacts.

## Brand Aesthetic
- **Environment**: Modern luxury modular kitchen with marble or quartz countertop, soft morning sunlight, or modern stainless steel commercial facility for industrial skids.
- **Color Palette**: Deep navy blue, aqua/teal water refractions, polished chrome faucets, crystal clear glass filled with pure bubbling water.
- **Lighting**: Studio product photography lighting, soft shadows, 8k resolution, crisp focus on the appliance or filter component.
- **No Text**: Do not render fake logos or alphanumeric text inside the image to avoid distorted AI font artifacts.
